# 02 · Brand look and voice

## Personality

A calm, expert studio partner, not a hype machine. It should feel like a sharp producer who has already done the work and shows you the results. Confident, warm, specific.

Three words: **clear, alive, trustworthy.**

## Visual direction

- **Feel:** editorial and modern. Light by default, with a full dark mode. Generous white space. The content (video thumbnails) is the hero; the interface stays quiet around it.
- **Avoid:** purple-to-blue "AI" gradients, glowing robots, sparkles on every button, neon cyberpunk.
- **Signature element:** a thin "loop" line motif (an open circle with an arrow) used for the learning loop, empty states and the logo placeholder.
- **Imagery:** vertical 9:16 video thumbnails with burned-in captions; real-feeling but clearly fictional people for the demo.

## Type

- Headings: **Inter Tight** (or Inter), 600. Sizes 32 / 24 / 20 / 16.
- Body: **Inter**, 400. 15px base, 13px for metadata.
- Numbers in metrics: tabular figures.

## Layout and components

- **Desktop first:** 1440px wide, left sidebar 240px, content max 1200px. 8px spacing grid. Card radius 12px, button radius 10px.
- **Mobile:** 390px wide for the approvals flow and the home summary.
- **Core components:** sidebar navigation; top bar with workspace switcher and "New" button; video card (9:16 thumbnail, hook text, platform icons, status chip, score); agent status pill (idle, working, waiting, blocked); metric tile with sparkline; funnel bar; calendar cell holding several post chips; compliance badge (passed, needs review, blocked); empty state with the loop motif.
- **Motion:** subtle. A progress shimmer on cuts being generated, a soft pulse on working agents.

## Voice (all product copy)

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Plain verbs: "Cut 12 clips from your podcast" | Jargon: "Leverage AI-powered content synergy" |
| Specific numbers: "3 posts today, next at 2:15 pm" | Vague hype: "Go viral now!" |
| Show what the agent did: "Trend Scout found 4 formats rising in coaching" | Pretend the AI is a person with feelings |
| Honest limits: "TikTok posts stay private until our app is approved" | Guarantees: "10x your followers" |
| Short sentences, sentence case | Title Case Everywhere, exclamation marks |

### Sample microcopy

- Empty upload: "Drop a long video. We'll find the moments worth posting."
- Multiplier done: "12 cuts ready. 3 need your eye before they post."
- Compliance block: "Blocked: this cut uses audio from the original video. Swap in a track from the platform library or use your own voice-over."
- Remake button: "Remake for my brand"
- Weekly report headline: "This week: 21 posts, 48,300 views, 214 link clicks, 6 discovery calls booked."
