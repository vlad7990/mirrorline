Architecture

The agent team: seven specialized intelligences working in one continuous loop.

A trend spotted on Monday becomes the client's own high-retention video, posted across three networks every single day, while verified conversion numbers steer the following week's editorial decisions.

01

Trend Scout Agent

Spots rising formats in coaching, leadership, and consulting niches. Deconstructs winning videos into formal structural recipes: hook, beats, pacing.

Output: Daily Recipe Card
02

Client Material Vault

Ingests long videos, keynote speeches, podcast audio, transcripts, and brand palettes. Guaranteed owned by the client with confirmed usage rights.

Output: Indexed Vector Clips
03

Remake Producer

Synthesizes the winning trend formula with Maya's authentic speaking footage and tone of voice. Produces compliant scripts with labelled AI scenes.

Output: Re-engineered Drafts
04

Multiplier Agent

One single source file becomes 8 to 15 distinct cuts. Crafts an alternative hook, duration (15s, 30s, 60s), and burned caption treatment for each.

Blocks copied footage, venue background music, wording collisions, or unauthorized likeness. Auto-applies AI disclosure tags and caps similarity below 30%.

Output: Cryptographic Clearance Log

Presents flagged clips in a streamlined, thumb-friendly mobile interface. You approve or edit in seconds, or enable autonomous mode once trust is earned.

Output: Explicit Authorizations
07

Schedules and uploads 3 times daily per platform across TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube. Always client-owned official APIs.

Output: Live Distribution

Frequently clarified questions

Can I review every single video before it goes live?

Yes. By default, every clip generated by the Multiplier and Remake agents lands in your "Needs You" queue or Mobile Companion for a single-tap signoff. You can switch trusted channels to autonomous posting at any time.

What happens if my original speech contains copyrighted conference music?

The Compliance Agent flags the cut as "Blocked", isolates your raw vocal track using neural speech separation, and replaces the background hall music with a cleared commercial track before asking for signoff.
