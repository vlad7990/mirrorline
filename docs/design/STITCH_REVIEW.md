# Stitch review: what to use, what to fix

Reviewed 9 November 2026.

## Use

- **S01–S10:** follow the Claude Design mockup closely, with better polish. Use them as visual references.
- **New screens:** sign-in and sign-up layouts (Google and Apple sign-in), the theme switch in Settings (light, dark, system), the mobile "All caught up" state (for later), and the marketing site structure (agent loop, funnel, FAQ).

## Do not use: replace or remove

1. **Testimonials and customer results:** the quote attributed to "Maya Torres, Managing Partner, Torres Advisory", star ratings, "4.8x qualified inbound", "$18,000 pipeline", "99.8% on-time daily drop". Remove them. Maya is fictional; label demo numbers "Sample data".
2. **Certifications and guarantees:** "SOC-2 Type II", "Mirrorline Certified Compliance Standard v4.2", "Guaranteed Compliance", "Guaranteed owned by the client", "Zero Hallucinations", "Zero copyright strikes", "100% intellectual property sovereignty". Remove them all.
3. **Features described as working that aren't built:** "audio licensed via TikTok Commercial API", automatic music replacement via "neural speech separation", "cryptographic consent", and "never used by public AI models". Remove them, or rephrase as plans ("We'll ask you to swap the audio").
4. **"Safe < 30%":** say "limit 30%". It's our internal rule, not a legal safe line.
5. **Pricing:** Stitch shows $650 / $1,500 / $3,200 and "Save 20%". Use Pilot $750 for 2 weeks, Growth $1,500/mo, Scale $3,000/mo, Studio self-serve "coming later". No annual discount.
6. **Names:** use `maya@mayatorres.example` (not torresadvisory.com). Maya is a leadership coach ("Maya Torres Coaching"), not an advisory firm. Replace "Apex Partners" with "a former employer".
7. **Brand and voice:** use the loop logo everywhere (not the M square or infinity mark). Replace jargon like "Studio master password", "Auth Node 04 Online", "Protocol v3.8" and "Studio Access · Secure Vault" with plain words ("Password", "Sign in").
