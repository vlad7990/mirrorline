# 10 — Sales focus: how we aim our own outreach

Written at Step 1 of `docs/prompts/MASTER_PROMPT_v4.md`. Internal. Nothing in
this file becomes a public page.

## 1. There is no industry focus

**We do not have a launch vertical, and we are not going to pick one.**

This reverses `01_PRODUCT_BRIEF.md`, which named coaches as "launch niche 1"
and local service businesses as "launch niche 2" (D38). The reason is
structural rather than strategic: the Niche Scan researches whatever niche an
account is in, so an industry focus would be a marketing constraint we do not
need the product to carry. The problems in `11 §2` are the same everywhere.
Only the niche changes, and the agents handle that per account.

What follows from that, and it is binding on every page, email and post:

- **No industry pages.** No `/coaches`, no `/clinics`, no `/real-estate`.
- **No industry-exclusive wording** anywhere a visitor can read — not "built
  for clinics", not "for coaches", not "the tool for trades". Step 7's
  banned-phrase CI test fails the build on this class of phrase.
- **No persona or industry featured above another.** Examples rotate through
  different kinds of business and individual, and none recurs often enough to
  read as the default customer.
- The starting recipes in `12 §2` are **internal seeds for the Trend Scout**.
  They are not target segments and they are not a content plan for the site.

## 2. We aim at account types and goals instead

The only segmentation, matching `13 §1` and `15 §2`. These are outreach
targets, not website sections.

| Account type | Where they already are | What we open with | Plan we aim at |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Business, existing account** | Has a page, posts rarely or generically; often has recorded material nobody has cut | Their own back catalogue is already a month of posts | Pilot → Growth or Scale |
| **Business, new account** | No presence on one or more platforms | We can start an account properly instead of with three posts and silence | Pilot → Growth |
| **Individual, existing audience** | Creator, expert or personal brand with long-form and some followers | They are producing the raw material already and posting a fraction of it | Creator |
| **Individual, new account** | Starting from zero | A repeatable series beats sporadic effort | Studio waitlist |

And by **goal** — leads, bookings, sales, audience growth, awareness,
community, or launching an account — because the goal decides which KPI we
lead the conversation with. A business chasing bookings wants to hear about
booked calls, not views. Same product, different first sentence.

## 3. How we reach them

- **We eat our own cooking.** Our own accounts run on Mirrorline, with posts
  aimed at different account types and goals. This is the main channel and the
  main proof: the service either grows our account or it does not.
- **The free Niche Scan is the offer**, in every channel, for everyone. It is
  the one thing worth clicking before anyone trusts us.
- **A warm list** of people who already know us, approached individually.
- **No cold-DM automation, no scraped lists, no bought contacts.** We are the
  honest alternative to a farm; outreach that imitates one would cost more
  than it earns.

## 4. What we may and may not say

Binding, and most of it already is:

- No guarantees — no promised views, followers, rankings, leads or revenue.
- No fake testimonials, ratings, client logos, certifications or invented
  numbers. When we have real client results, they go up with permission and
  with the method stated.
- **No competitor brand names.** We name categories — clipping tools,
  agencies, doing it yourself, repost pages — and we paraphrase complaints
  rather than quoting reviewers. The sources stay in `11`.
- The market research figures in `11` are **directional and must be re-checked
  before any of them is quoted publicly.** That file says so itself.
- The investigation that led to this company is not a sales asset. It does not
  appear on `/about` or in outreach.
- Studio is a **waitlist**, not a product on sale. Nothing implies otherwise.

## 5. Who we turn down

Political content, gambling, crypto, adult content, and anyone under 18 —
listed publicly on `/legal/acceptable-use`.

Regulated niches — health, finance, legal, real estate — are **sold to
normally**, with the rule pack switched on automatically and shown to the
client. We say this during the sale rather than after it: the rule pack is a
reason to trust us, not a caveat to bury.
