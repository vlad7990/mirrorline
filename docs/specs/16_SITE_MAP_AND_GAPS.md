# 16 — Site map: what we have and what's missing

Checked against the repo on 12 Nov 2026 (apps/web/app).

## 1. Positioning change: industry-agnostic

Mirrorline works for **any business or individual, any industry, new or existing account**. We don't build industry pages. Our agents research each account's niche (Niche Scan), so the site sells the system rather than a vertical. The only choices a visitor makes are:
- **Who:** a business or an individual
- **Account:** starting new, or growing an existing one
- **Goal:** leads, bookings, sales, audience, awareness, community, or launching an account

Industry is asked for in onboarding, and only so the agents can research it.
