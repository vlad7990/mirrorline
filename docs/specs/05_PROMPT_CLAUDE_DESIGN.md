# 05 · Prompt for Claude Design

Attach files 01, 02, 03 and 08 to the conversation, then paste the prompt below.

---

Design a clickable, high-fidelity mockup of a web app called **Mirrorline** (placeholder name). Mirrorline is an AI content studio for coaches and local businesses. It turns the client's own long videos, and formats trending in their niche, into original short videos. A team of seven AI agents then posts them several times a day to the client's own Instagram, TikTok and YouTube accounts, and learns from the results.

**Use the attached files as the source of truth:**
- 01_PRODUCT_BRIEF.md for what the product does and its rules
- 02_BRAND_AND_VOICE.md for colours, type, components and the tone of every word
- 03_SCREENS_SPEC.md for each screen's purpose, layout, components and states
- 08_MOCK_DATA.md for all names, numbers, hooks and posts, so every screen tells the same story

**Deliver:**
1. A small design system first: colour tokens (light and dark), type scale, buttons, inputs, chips, status pills, the video card, metric tile, calendar post chip, compliance badge, agent card, and the empty state with the loop motif.
2. Ten desktop screens at 1440px: S1 Onboarding (show step 1, with steps 2 and 3 as states), S2 Home, S3 Upload and multiply, S4 Trend board, S5 Remake studio, S6 Calendar and approvals, S7 Results, S8 Agents, S9 Brand kit, S10 Settings and billing.
3. Two mobile screens at 390px: M1 Approvals (swipe) and M2 Home summary.
4. Clickable links along the demo path: S1 → S2 → S3 → S4 → S5 → S6 → S7 → S8.
5. At least one empty, loading, error and compliance-blocked state.

**Rules:**
- Video thumbnails are vertical 9:16 with burned-in captions, showing fictional people only. No real creators, logos of real brands, or real people's faces.
- The trend board shows a format as an abstract storyboard of beats, never as another creator's video.
- No hype language, no guaranteed results, no purple AI gradients.
- Every AI-generated scene shows a small "AI" label.

After the screens, give me a short list of the design decisions you made and anything in the spec you think should change.
