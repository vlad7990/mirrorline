# 01 · Product brief

## One line

Mirrorline turns a brand's own videos, and the formats going viral in its niche, into a steady stream of original short videos, published several times a day to the brand's own accounts by a team of AI agents that learns from every post.

## Who it is for

| Segment | Example | Why they buy |
|---|---|---|
| Coaches and consultants (launch niche 1) | Leadership coach with a weekly podcast | Hours of video nobody watches; one client pays for months of service |
| Local service businesses (launch niche 2) | Med spa, gym, dental clinic | Need steady local reach; each booking is worth $100–$500 |
| Product brands (later) | Beauty, supplements, home goods | Need volume of content and ad creative |
| Creators (self-serve, later) | Creators with 1K–100K followers | Want to post daily without an editor |

## What the product does

1. **Multiply:** one long video (podcast, webinar, talk, demo) becomes 8–15 genuinely different short cuts: a new hook, length or format in each, captioned in the brand style.
2. **Remake trends:** the Trend Scout finds formats rising in the niche and nearby niches. The Remake Producer then makes the brand's own original version within 48 hours, using a new script, the brand's footage and labelled AI scenes.
3. **Post daily:** the Publisher posts several times a day on Instagram, TikTok and YouTube Shorts, through official APIs, to accounts the client owns.
4. **Learn:** the Performance Analyst reads views, clicks and sales per post and feeds the winners back to the Scout and the Producer.
5. **Report:** a weekly report runs from views to clicks to leads or sales.

## Unique value proposition

Any format that goes viral, in any niche, becomes the brand's own original video within 48 hours, multiplied into many posts and published daily to the brand's own accounts. Every post makes the next one better.

## Non-negotiable rules (shown in the product, enforced in code)

- **Inputs:** only the client's own material, or footage we hold the rights to.
- **Viral remakes copy the format only:** idea, structure, pacing, hook pattern. Never another creator's footage, audio, exact wording, face, voice or name.
- **Publishing:** only through official platform APIs, to accounts the client connected and can disconnect.
- **AI labels:** AI-generated scenes are labelled, using each platform's AI label where one exists.
- **Consent:** no face-swaps. Voice clones and likeness only with a signed release.
- **No near-duplicates:** each post is a different cut.
- **Promises:** no guaranteed views or followers, anywhere in the product or the marketing.

## Success metrics

- Client side: posts published on time, views per post, link clicks, leads or sales per month, first-draft approval rate.
- Our side: monthly recurring revenue, pilot-to-retainer conversion, delivery cost per client, churn.
