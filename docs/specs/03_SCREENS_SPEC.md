# 03 · Screens

Ten desktop screens (1440 wide) and two mobile screens (390 wide). Every screen uses the demo data in 08_MOCK_DATA.md.

## Demo path (the 3-minute sales demo)

S1 Onboarding → S2 Home → S3 Upload and multiply → S4 Trend board → S5 Remake studio → S6 Calendar and approvals → S7 Results → S8 Agents

Navigation in the sidebar: Home, Create (Upload, Trends), Calendar, Results, Agents, Brand kit, Settings and billing.

---

## S1 · Onboarding (3 steps)

**Purpose:** get a new client from sign-up to their first cuts in under 10 minutes.

- **Step 1, brand kit:** upload logo; pick colours (auto-suggested from the logo); fonts; a "how you talk" box with 3 tone sliders (formal to casual, calm to energetic, expert to friendly); and a sample caption preview that updates live.
- **Step 2, connect accounts:** cards for Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and LinkedIn with "Connect" buttons (OAuth). Note under TikTok: "Posts stay private until our app is approved by TikTok." A checkbox: "I own or have rights to everything I upload."
- **Step 3, niche and goal:** niche dropdown (Coaching), nearby niches as chips (Productivity, Leadership, Wellness); main goal (Book discovery calls); link to send traffic to; posts per day per platform (default 2).
- Progress indicator across the top. Final button: "Start my first upload."

## S2 · Home (command centre)

**Purpose:** the client sees in 5 seconds what happened today and what needs them.

- **Top row of metric tiles:** posts this week (21), views (48.3K), link clicks (214), calls booked (6). Each tile has a 7-day sparkline.
- **"Needs you" panel:** 3 cuts awaiting approval, 1 blocked by compliance, with buttons.
- **"Today" timeline:** posts scheduled per platform with times and status (published, scheduled, needs review).
- **Agent activity feed:** live lines such as "Trend Scout found 'The 3-word reframe' rising in Leadership (+340% this week)" and "Multiplier cut 12 clips from Episode 47".
- **Learning loop card:** "This week's winner: question hooks, 2.1× average views. Next week we're making more of them."

## S3 · Upload and multiply

**Purpose:** show the core magic: one video becomes many.

- **Left:** a drag-and-drop zone, plus "Import from my YouTube channel." The source shows as "Episode 47: Why good managers burn out (52 min)."
- **Progress steps:** Transcribing → Finding moments → Cutting → Captioning → Compliance check, each with a status.
- **Results grid:** 12 vertical cut cards. Each shows the thumbnail with captions, a hook line, length (0:18 to 0:58), format tag (Hook clip, Story, Quote card, Carousel, Reply), a predicted strength score, and a compliance badge.
- **Card actions:** preview, edit hook, regenerate, approve, schedule.
- **Top-right summary:** "12 cuts ready · 9 passed · 3 need your eye."

## S4 · Trend board

**Purpose:** show trends from the niche and nearby niches, ready to remake.

- **Filters:** My niche / Nearby niches / All; platform; time (24h, 7d).
- **Trend cards (8):** format name ("The 3-word reframe"), niche it came from, growth (+340%), and an anonymised mini storyboard of 3–4 beats as simple frames with labels, not the original video. Also: why it works (one line), fit score for this brand, and the button "Remake for my brand".
- **Banner:** "We remake the format, never the footage. Every remake is original."

## S5 · Remake studio

**Purpose:** prove the remake is original and on-brand.

- **Left column, format recipe:** beats with timings (Hook 0–2s, Problem 2–8s, Reframe 8–20s, Proof 20–35s, Call to action 35–40s), pacing and caption style.
- **Middle column, our version:** a new script in the brand voice, a shot list mixing client footage and labelled AI scenes, and the chosen voice (the client's own recording, or a stock voice).
- **Right column, preview:** a 9:16 player with the result, plus 3 alternative hooks to A/B.
- **Bottom, compliance panel:** a checklist with ticks: no original footage, no original audio, new wording (similarity 12%, limit 30%), AI scenes labelled, no real person's likeness. Status: Passed.
- **Actions:** Approve and multiply (makes 5 variants), Edit, Discard.

## S6 · Calendar and approvals

**Purpose:** show daily posting, several times a day, under the client's control.

- **Week view:** columns for days, rows for platforms. Each cell holds 2–3 post chips with time, thumbnail and status colour.
- **Right drawer:** the selected post with preview, caption, hashtags, link, and an AI label toggle (locked on for AI scenes).
- **Approval mode switch:** "Approve each post" or "Auto-approve after 24h unless I object."
- **Batch bar:** "Approve all 9 for tomorrow."

## S7 · Results

**Purpose:** connect content to money.

- **Funnel:** views 48,300 → profile visits 1,930 → link clicks 214 → leads 31 → calls booked 6 (last 7 days).
- **Top posts table:** thumbnail, hook, platform, views, clicks, calls.
- **What's working:** hook types ranked, best posting times heatmap, formats ranked.
- **Download weekly report (PDF)** button.

## S8 · Agents

**Purpose:** make the AI team visible and trustworthy.

- **Seven agent cards:** Trend Scout, Remake Producer, Multiplier, Compliance, Client Concierge, Publisher, Performance Analyst. Each shows its status pill, last action, items done this week, spend this month against its cap, and an on/off toggle.
- **Loop diagram** at the top, showing how the agents hand work to each other.
- **Click an agent:** a log of recent actions with timestamps and an explanation of each decision.

## S9 · Brand kit

Logo, colours, fonts, caption styles (3 presets with previews), intro and outro templates, voice settings (stock voice, or "my own voice" with a consent status: release signed on 9 Nov 2026), and words to always and never use.

## S10 · Settings and billing

Connected accounts with status and disconnect buttons; plan card (Growth, $1,500/month, 90 posts); usage meter (63 of 90 posts used); invoices; team members; data and rights (rights confirmations, consent releases, export my data).

---

## Mobile M1 · Approvals (swipe)

Full-screen 9:16 preview with caption. Swipe right to approve, left to send back with a reason chip (wrong tone, wrong fact, don't like the hook), or tap to edit the hook. Counter at the top: "3 to review."

## Mobile M2 · Home summary

Today's metric tiles, the next 3 scheduled posts, and the "Needs you" items.

## States to show at least once

Empty (no uploads yet, using the loop motif), loading (cuts generating, with a shimmer), error (account disconnected, "Reconnect Instagram"), blocked by compliance (S3 card), success (published toast).
