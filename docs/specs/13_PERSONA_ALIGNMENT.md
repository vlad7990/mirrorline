# 13 — Who we serve: account types and goals (industry-agnostic)

Mirrorline serves **everyone**: any business or individual, in any industry, with a new or existing account. We don't segment by industry. Our agents research each account's niche, so the product adapts to the account rather than the account choosing an industry page.

## 1. Account types (the only segmentation on the site)

| Account type | Typical starting point | Best plan | Input mix | Posts per week |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Business, existing account | Has a page but posts rarely or generically | Pilot $750 → Growth $1,500 or Scale $3,000 | Their footage + remakes | 7–21 |
| Business, new account | No presence on one or more platforms | Pilot → Growth | Mostly created videos at first, then their footage | 7–14 |
| Individual, existing audience | Creator, expert or personal brand with footage and some followers | Creator $499 | Their long-form + remakes | 14–28 |
| Individual, new account | Starting from zero | Studio $79 (from month 2; waitlist now) | Mostly created videos + phone clips | 7–14 |

## 2. Goals decide the metrics, not industry

Every account picks a main goal: leads, bookings, sales, audience growth, awareness, community, or launching a new account. The KPI set, targets and dashboard follow that goal (see 15_METRICS_AND_KPIS.md).

## 3. How the agents adapt to any industry

1. Onboarding asks for the industry and niche in the user's own words (free text, plus up to 3 competitor or inspiration handles).
2. Trend Scout runs the Niche Scan (12 §1) and builds this account's format recipes and benchmarks.
3. If the niche is regulated (health, finance, legal, real estate), the matching rule pack is switched on automatically, and the user can review it.
4. Performance Analyst sets targets from the 14-day baseline plus Niche Scan benchmarks.

## Rules for copy and UI

- No industry is featured above another. Examples across the site rotate through different kinds of businesses and individuals.
- No guarantees, invented testimonials, ratings or logos. Sample data is labelled "Sample data".
- Not served: political, gambling, crypto, adult, anyone under 18.
