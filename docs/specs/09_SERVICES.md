## 1. The two ways in

Every account uses one or both. The mix is set at onboarding from the account
type (13 §1) and can change at any time.

- **You send.** The client sends their own long videos — podcasts, webinars,
  talks, demos, phone clips. The Multiplier turns one of them into many
  genuinely different cuts.
- **We create.** We remake formats that are growing in the client's niche. We
  copy the **structure only** — hook, setup, payoff, call to action — never
  another creator's footage, audio, wording, face, voice or name. New script,
  new footage, the client's own voice. AI scenes are always labelled.

A new account usually starts mostly on "we create" and shifts towards its own
footage as it builds a library. Neither way costs more than the other.

## 2. The seven agents

One loop: what Trend Scout finds on Monday becomes the client's own videos,
posted all week, and the results shape next week. Each agent is a worker with
its own prompt, tools, spending cap and log, and every action it takes is
written down and visible. The metric each one owns is in 15 §6.

| # | Agent | What it does for the client |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Trend Scout** | Runs the Niche Scan and keeps it fresh: what is growing in this niche, and why |
| 2 | **Remake Producer** | Turns a proven format into the client's own original video |
| 3 | **Multiplier** | Turns one of the client's videos into many distinct cuts |
| 4 | **Compliance** | Checks every video before it can be scheduled, including the Originality Gate |
| 5 | **Client Concierge** | Approvals, questions, and the weekly report |
| 6 | **Publisher** | Posts several times a day to the client's own accounts, through official APIs |
| 7 | **Performance Analyst** | Sets the baseline and targets, calculates KPIs, raises alerts, writes next week's plan |

## 3. The Niche Scan

The free lead magnet, and the first thing that happens on every account. It is
how an industry-agnostic product stays specific to the account in front of it.

**What it finds:** who is growing in this niche and *why*, turned into format
recipes we can remake. Ranked by **views per follower and consistency over 30
days**, not follower count — a 4,000-follower account getting 40,000 views a
video is a better model than a two-million account coasting.

**What the client gets:** 5–10 format recipes ranked by fit score, each with
why it is growing and how we would make it theirs, plus benchmarks that seed
their targets.

**Where it looks:** TikTok Creative Center, the Meta Ad Library, the YouTube
Data API, the client's own insights through official APIs, and a strategist's
manual review of up to 30 public examples.

**What it never does:** scrape private data, log into anyone else's account,
or download or store other creators' videos or audio. We keep **links and
descriptions only — never media** (12 §1).

## 4. Calendar with approvals

Everything we make lands on a calendar before it lands on a platform. The
client approves each post, or switches on auto-approve after 24 hours and
objects to anything they do not want. Approving a week takes about fifteen
minutes. Nothing posts that has not passed Compliance.

## 5. Daily posting to the client's own accounts

Several posts a day across Instagram, TikTok and YouTube Shorts, at the times
that work for that account, through **official platform APIs** to accounts the
client connected and can disconnect at any moment.

Stated plainly because it is true and clients find out anyway: **TikTok posts
stay private until our app is audited by TikTok.** We start well below the
platforms' own rate limits and raise cadence as an account proves it.

No device automation, no app clones, no proxies, no purchased or farmed
accounts. Ever.

## 6. The Originality Gate and the compliance checks

Every video is checked before it can be scheduled. The checks are: no original
footage, no original audio, new wording against the source and against the
client's own last 60 posts, rights confirmed, consent on file for any voice or
likeness, and the AI label set wherever generated material appears.

**The Originality Gate blocks and rewrites. It never perturbs content to get
it under a threshold.** That distinction is the whole product: the technique
that used to be run to evade detection is run backwards here, to enforce
originality. A blocked item comes back rewritten, with the reason shown.

## 7. Metrics and KPIs on every account

Per `15_METRICS_AND_KPIS.md`, and **every account gets this, on every plan.**
Only the portfolio view is plan-gated.

Each account picks a main goal — leads, bookings, sales, audience growth,
awareness, community, or launching a new account — and the KPI set, targets
and dashboard follow the **goal**, never the industry. Targets are proposed
after a 14-day baseline plus the Niche Scan's benchmarks, and the client edits
and approves them.

Seven KPI tiers, the same for everyone: Production, Content health, Growth,
Outcome, Return, Quality and safety, Agent operations. Custom KPIs can be
added by hand or by CSV — "phone calls", "walk-ins", "Google reviews".

**The honesty rules are part of the service, not the fine print.** Targets are
called targets and never guarantees. Projections are dashed and labelled. A
source that is not connected shows "Not connected" and a connect button —
never a zero. Every number shows how fresh it is.

## 8. The weekly Results Ledger

One table a week, per account: posts, views, sends, saves, follows, leads,
bookings and sales. Exportable as CSV and PDF, and sent by the Client
Concierge with the Performance Analyst's note — what worked, what we will
change, and what we need from the client.

It is the proof format for the whole service. It runs from views all the way
to money, rather than stopping at views.

## 9. Repost Watch

When someone else reposts a client's video, the platforms' own protection
tools mostly reach monetised creators first and leave smaller accounts
exposed. So we crosspost originals to the client's own Facebook page to make
them eligible, and give them a checklist for enrolling and acting.

**We never file claims on a client's behalf.** That is their decision and
their legal standing, not ours.

## 10. Plain billing

The direct answer to P3, and it is a feature.

- **Flat monthly price.** No credits, no per-minute charges, no setup fee.
- **Cancel in one click**, from Settings, at any time. No contract, no notice
  period, no cancellation call.
- **The client keeps every file, forever.** Export everything at any time,
  including after cancelling.
- **No charges after cancelling.** The plan runs to the end of the period
  already paid for, then stops.

## 12. Who we serve

**Everyone.** Any business or individual, in any industry, with a new or an
existing account. We do not build industry pages and no industry, persona or
profession is featured above another; examples across the site rotate through
different kinds of business and individual.

Regulated niches — health, finance, legal, real estate — are served, and the
matching **rule pack** is switched on automatically and shown to the client
for review.

**Not served:** political content, gambling, crypto, adult content, and anyone
under 18.
